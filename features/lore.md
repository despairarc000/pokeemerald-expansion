# Lore

**Core line:** Not story locked

The world has a hidden past that the player pieces together from things they see. See `design/lore-implicit.md` for the rules and a proposed hidden truth (not decided).

| Ver | Adds | Requires | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One environmental clue in Meadowmere (an old boundary stone or a worn carving), no text, no reward required | regions v1 | Parked |
| 2 | A repeating motif appears across places | v1, regions v2 | Parked |
| 3 | Local folk beliefs that differ between places and can contradict each other | v2, NPC life v1 | Parked |
| 4 | Clue chains inside a region (ruin, object, creature behavior that fit together) | v3, rare finds v2 | Parked |
| 5 | An explicit lore layer (readable texts, characters who tell stories), never contradicting the hidden truth | v4 | Parked |

## Notes and parked ideas

- Clues never gate anything.
- Every clue has a physical cause in the world's history.
- The hidden truth is a proposal and needs the owner's decision before it is used.
