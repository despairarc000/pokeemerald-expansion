# Fainting

**Core line:** Semi survival (you don't die, but you manage your way of life)

No respawn. No game-over screen. The world keeps going, and what happens to you has a cause that exists in the world.

| Ver | Adds | Requires | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No respawn, no game over. Fainting ends the encounter and the game continues | nothing | Next |
| 2 | You faint, you lose time and resources | world time v1, hunger v1 | Parked |
| 3 | You wake up near where you fell, because the danger left | wild Pokemon v2 (behaviors) | Parked |
| 4 | Or someone found you and took you somewhere safe | NPC life v2 (routines) | Parked |
| 5 | Or something bad found you (a hostile person or Pokemon) | NPC life and wild Pokemon behaviors | Parked |

## Next version spec (v1)

- There is no respawn point and no "you blacked out" teleport.
- When the whole party is down, the encounter ends and play continues where you are.
- What the world does around you is not designed yet. Until v2 to v4 exist, the honest behavior is the simplest one that does not fake a rescue.
- Exact v1 behavior is decided when we reach it, within this rule: no invented rescuer, no invented safe room.

## Notes and parked ideas

- A rescue only happens if an NPC's real routine crosses your path. Nobody passing by means nobody comes.
- Faint deep in a dangerous region costs more than faint in a busy town. This follows from the world, not from a rule.
- NPCs remembering a rescue, being robbed, region-specific rescuers: all parked.
- Open: how to avoid repeated faints when a threat is still near, without a scripted escape.
