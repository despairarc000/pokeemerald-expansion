## Next version spec (v1)

- Player only. Three states, changing slowly with in-game time.
- Berries (Emerald already has berry items and trees) restore hunger.
- Starving means weaker stats and slower recovery, never death and never a lost save.
- Exact numbers decided when built, and kept small.

## Notes and parked ideas

- "Semi" survival: this must not dominate play.
