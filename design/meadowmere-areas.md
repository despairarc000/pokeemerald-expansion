# Meadowmere: areas (one level down)

Detail for each of the seven areas in `design/region-meadowmere.md`. An area is a natural stretch of land. Places (villages, farms, shrines) sit inside areas and get their own detail later, one at a time.

**Status:** a proposal by the AI author for the owner. Background design, not a commitment. Follows the world feel rules in `features/regions-and-exploration.md`: barriers are costs, not locks, no artificial gates, paths follow the land. Map counts and sizes are guesses, not measured.

**Lore is not here.** Each area has a number of \"curiosity slots\" that the author fills later. They are not listed on purpose.

- The Commons is the heart, and every other area is a short walk from it.
- The river runs from the northwest down the west side, bends through the River Bend, and leaves south toward the coast.
- The road network meets at Wickerby, at the south end of the Low Meadows.

## 1. The Commons

- **Position:** the center. The start (Fairhaven) is here.
- **Terrain:** gently rolling, well-drained, a patchwork of greens, gardens and small paddocks.
- **Key spots:** the village green with its well, a handful of lanes, a washing place at a stream, a small chapel-like shelter, a communal bread oven, an unfenced orchard row, a duck pond.
- **Paths:** worn tracks fan out from the green to every other area. No paved main street.
- **Look:** the coziest and most colorful area. Flower boxes, laundry lines, thatch and clay tile, hay carts.
- **Life:** the tamest creatures: grazers on the paddocks, birds and small ground-dwellers, a few pets and working animals.
- **Food:** gardens and berry bushes everywhere. The easiest food in the world.
- **Traversal:** low terraces and garden walls, climbable. Flat and easy.
- **Danger:** Safe.
- **People:** the densest population in the region. Gardeners, bakers, children, elders, a few traders passing through.
- **Curiosity slots:** 3.
- **Map plan (guess):** 3 outdoor maps, plus interiors for homes and a few shops.

## 2. The River Bend

- **Position:** west of the Commons, where the river turns.
- **Terrain:** slow river, reed beds, gravel bars, mud banks, a few islands of willows.
- **Key spots:** the ford (shallow stepping route), a wooden footbridge, a watermill (Hollowmill), a fish weir, a small ferry post that is rarely used, mossy stones at the water's edge.
- **Paths:** tracks follow the banks. Crossing is possible only at the ford, the footbridge, or by swimming where deep. Nothing is walled.
- **Look:** soft blues and silver, mist at dawn, dragonfly and reed motion.
- **Life:** water and amphibious creatures, birds at the reeds, shy river creatures at dusk.
- **Food:** fish, water plants, riverside berries.
- **Traversal:** river crossing by ford, bridge or swimming. Currents are gentle but real. Deep water costs effort and carries risk.
- **Danger:** Calm. Deep pools hold shy but territorial water life, signaled by ripples.
- **People:** millers, fishers, a ferryman, a few anglers.
- **Curiosity slots:** 3.
- **Map plan (guess):** 3 outdoor maps, plus the mill interior.

## 3. The Orchard Terraces

- **Position:** north of the Commons on south-facing slopes.
- **Terrain:** stepped hillside terraces held by low stone walls, with fruit trees and berry hedges.
- **Key spots:** the pressing house, a bee yard, terrace stairs, an orchard keeper's hut, a view platform at the top.
- **Paths:** switchback tracks and terrace steps. Every wall can be climbed.
- **Look:** the brightest area. Blossom and ripe fruit, bees and butterflies, warm stone.
- **Life:** pollinators and fruit-eaters in numbers. Gentle, curious, drawn to berries.
- **Food:** the richest berry and fruit source in the region.
- **Traversal:** the best place to learn climbing: many small, safe ledges.
- **Danger:** Safe to Calm. Wasps and territorial insects near the bee yard, signaled by humming.
- **People:** orchard keepers, pickers, a cider maker.
- **Curiosity slots:** 3.
- **Map plan (guess):** 3 outdoor maps, plus the pressing house.

## 4. The Hedgerow Wood

- **Position:** northwest, between the Commons, the river and the hills.
- **Terrain:** old hedgerows grown into a thick maze of thorn, bramble and crooked trees, with small clearings and hidden dells.
- **Key spots:** a handful of clearings, a half-buried gate in a hedge, a rough shelter, a stream crossing, the thickest knot at the center.
- **Paths:** narrow animal tracks and one woodcutter's path. Easy to lose your way, never blocked outright.
- **Look:** darker greens, dappled light, quieter. Slightly eerie, never frightening.
- **Life:** shy forest creatures. One territorial animal guards the center. Tracks and sudden quiet signal it.
- **Food:** wild berries, nuts, mushrooms, though harder to find.
- **Traversal:** slow going. The maze costs time and can get you lost. Hedges can be pushed through slowly. No walls.
- **Danger:** Calm at the edges, a small Wild pocket at the center.
- **People:** the occasional woodcutter or forager. Few.
- **Curiosity slots:** 4 (the most in the region).
- **Map plan (guess):** 3 outdoor maps.

## 5. The Low Meadows

- **Position:** east and south of the Commons.
- **Terrain:** wide, flat, open grass with wildflowers, ponds and scattered trees.
- **Key spots:** Wickerby market town at the southern crossroads, Thistledown's farms, grazing fields, a windbreak of tall trees, a roadside well, a pond with stepping stones.
- **Paths:** the main roads: south to Neonport, east to the Downs, west to the River Bend. Lots of open ground in between.
- **Look:** big sky, long views, wind in grass, bright flowers.
- **Life:** herds and flocks of grazers, birds, rabbits and burrowers, pollinators on the flowers.
- **Food:** grain, fruit, pasture, garden produce.
- **Traversal:** easy, flat. Ponds and ditches are the only real obstacles.
- **Danger:** Calm. Large grazers can be territorial when startled, and their tracks signal it.
- **People:** farmers, herders, market traders, travelers on the roads.
- **Curiosity slots:** 3.
- **Map plan (guess):** 4 outdoor maps, plus Wickerby's interiors.

## 6. The Downs

- **Position:** east of the Low Meadows, rising toward the Steppe.
- **Terrain:** rolling grass hills, exposed ridges, scattered standing rocks and thorn thickets, thin soil.
- **Key spots:** a ridge track with long views, a shepherd's hut, a stone-ringed spring, a lone tree on the highest hill, wind-bent hawthorn.
- **Paths:** ridge and valley tracks. Fewer than in the Low Meadows. Exposed in bad weather.
- **Look:** golden-green, wide skies, wind. Fewer buildings, more openness.
- **Life:** sturdy grazers, birds of the open air, ground nesters, fast runners that live on open ground.
- **Food:** sparse. Wild berries in sheltered folds.
- **Traversal:** gentle slopes, a few steep scarps, climbable. Gusts matter once flight exists.
- **Danger:** Calm, and the first real step toward Wild at the far east edge.
- **People:** shepherds, a few walkers heading to or from the Steppe.
- **Curiosity slots:** 3.
- **Map plan (guess):** 3 outdoor maps.

## 7. The Foothill Edge

- **Position:** the whole north border.
- **Terrain:** soil thins and turns to scree and low cliffs. Meadow gives way to heather and stone. Streams run down from the hills.
- **Key spots:** the first cairn on the northern track, a quarry scar, a stream cascade, a cave mouth that leads up toward Cragmoor, a high pasture.
- **Paths:** the northern track climbs to Cragmoor. Elsewhere the edge is open to anyone willing to climb.
- **Look:** cooler colors, grey stone, heather, thinner air.
- **Life:** hardy climbers and rock dwellers, a few shy mountain creatures wandering down.
- **Food:** scarce. Hardy berries and roots.
- **Traversal:** steeper ground and short cliffs. Climbing is faster here with a partner.
- **Danger:** Calm to Wild toward the north, signaled by rockfall marks and tracks.
- **People:** a few herders and quarry workers.
- **Curiosity slots:** 3.
- **Map plan (guess):** 3 outdoor maps.
