# Meadowmere: places (one more level down)

Detail for each place inside the areas in `design/meadowmere-areas.md`. A place is a village, town, farm, shrine or shelter. Interiors (homes, shops) are listed per place, not designed yet.

**Status:** a proposal by the AI author for the owner. Background design, not a commitment. Follows the world feel rules in `features/regions-and-exploration.md` and the core in `design/CORE.md`. Map and interior counts are guesses, not measured.

**Lore is not here.** Each place has \"curiosity slots\" that the author fills later. They are not listed on purpose.

## Main places

### Fairhaven (village, in the Commons)

The start. A farming village that feels lived in, not planned.

- **Layout:** about a dozen homes in a loose, uneven ring around a village green. A stone well in the middle. Lanes wander between homes and gardens. Paths leave the village in four directions: a north track up to the Orchard Terraces, an east lane to the Low Meadows, a west track to the River Bend, and a south road toward Wickerby.
- **Civic and shops:** a bakery with a shared stone oven, a larder (the general store, run on barter and coin), a healer's cottage (a small clinic and rest room), a meeting house, a washing place at a stream, a small smithy, a stable and barn.
- **Look:** thatch and clay tile, whitewashed walls, flower boxes, laundry lines, hay carts, an unfenced orchard row, a duck pond.
- **Rhythm:** bakers up at first light, the green busy mid-morning, quiet at midday, a gathering at the well in the evening. These are routines for later, not scripts.
- **Danger:** Safe. Wildlife wanders through the edges and gardens.
- **Food:** the easiest in the region. Gardens everywhere.
- **Rest and healing:** the healer's cottage is the place to recover. How healing works is a design choice not yet made (see open questions).
- **Curiosity slots:** 3.
- **Map plan (guess):** 1 main outdoor map, about 32 by 32. Interiors: bakery, larder, healer's cottage, meeting house, and 3 or 4 homes (about 8).

### Wickerby (market town, south of the Low Meadows)

The busiest place in the region, where the roads meet.

- **Layout:** a crossroads with a wide market square at the center. Roads run in from every direction. A covered market, a cart yard, a weighing house, grain stores along the south edge, a notice board at the square.
- **Civic and shops:** a coaching inn (the best place to rest and eat), a trader's yard (supplies and goods), a bigger healer's house, a guildhall, stables, a few craft shops (rope, pottery, tools).
- **Look:** busy and colorful. Striped awnings, stacked crates, hanging lanterns, hand-painted signs, wheel ruts in the dust.
- **Rhythm:** a market on a set day, travelers arriving and leaving, carts at dawn. Routines for later.
- **Danger:** Safe.
- **Food:** plentiful and varied. Cooked food and rare ingredients can be found here.
- **Connections:** the road south leads toward Neonport. East leads to the Downs. West leads to the River Bend. North leads to the Commons.
- **Curiosity slots:** 3.
- **Map plan (guess):** 2 outdoor maps. Interiors: coaching inn, trader, healer, guildhall, and 4 or 5 shops and homes (about 9).

### Hollowmill (mill hamlet, in the River Bend)

A small, quiet place on the river with a mill at its heart.

- **Layout:** a watermill with its wheel and race, the miller's house, a handful of fishers' huts on the bank, drying racks, a fish weir, the ford and the footbridge nearby.
- **Look:** soft blues and silver, the mill wheel turning, nets and racks, mist at dawn.
- **Rhythm:** the mill grinds in the morning, fishers go out early, the hamlet is quiet by evening.
- **Danger:** Safe in the hamlet, Calm along the river.
- **Food:** fish, ground grain, riverside berries.
- **Curiosity slots:** 3.
- **Map plan (guess):** 1 outdoor map shared with the River Bend maps. Interiors: the mill, the miller's house, one fisher's hut (about 3).

### Thistledown (scattered farms, between the Low Meadows and the Downs)

A handful of farmsteads, each with its own garden, animals and windbreak.

- **Layout:** four or five farms spread over the land, a shared well, a sheltered lane, a windbreak of tall trees, a paddock with grazing animals, a hay barn.
- **Look:** golden hay, long fences, kitchen gardens, weathered timber.
- **Rhythm:** work from dawn, midday shade, evening smoke from chimneys.
- **Danger:** Safe in the farms, Calm around them.
- **Food:** grain, vegetables, eggs, animal produce.
- **Curiosity slots:** 3.
- **Map plan (guess):** 2 outdoor maps. Interiors: three farmhouses (about 3).
