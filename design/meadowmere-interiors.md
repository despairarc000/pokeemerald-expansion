# Meadowmere: interiors

Detail for every interior in `design/meadowmere-places.md`. An interior is the inside of a building: a home, shop, mill or hut. Each is one engine map.

**Status:** a proposal by the AI author for the owner. Background design, not a commitment. Sizes and counts are guesses, not measured. Follows `design/CORE.md` and the world feel rules in `features/regions-and-exploration.md`.

**Lore is not here.** Each interior may hold one small unexplained detail, placed by the author later. They are not listed on purpose.

## How design detail is layered (and where interiors sit)

Interiors are not the smallest grain. The full ladder is:

| Level | What it is | State |
|---|---|---|
| World | The whole game | Designed (high level) |
| Region | One of the 14 regions | Meadowmere brief written |
| Area | A natural stretch of a region | Meadowmere areas written |
| Place | A village, farm, shrine, shelter | Meadowmere places written |
| **Site** | **One building or interior (one engine map)** | **This document** |
| Space | A room or zone inside a site (kitchen, workshop) | This document, in words |
| Feature | A prop, piece of furniture, person or interaction spot | Later, placed when the map is built |
| Tile | A single 16 by 16 metatile | Built from the tile art |

This document goes down to the **space** level: what the rooms are for, who is usually there, and what stands out. It does not place individual furniture. That happens when each map is built.

## Principles for every interior

1. **A reason to exist.** Each interior shows how people live or work: food stores, tools, beds, a craft.
2. **No exposition.** Nobody explains anything. Details tell the story.
3. **One thing to do.** Each interior offers at least one real thing: rest, buy, trade, craft, listen, look.
4. **Clear exits.** Doors lead where they should, and the way out is always obvious.
5. **Lived in.** A little untidy. Real objects in real places, not rows of identical furniture.
6. **Survival-aware.** Food and rest show up in homes and shops, because everyone eats.
7. **Quiet life.** People have routines. These are for later, not scripted.

Size classes are guesses: small about 8 by 7 tiles, medium about 12 by 9, large about 16 by 12.

## Tiles for interiors

New tiles will be generated (by the owner's chosen tool) rather than relying only on the existing Emerald tilesets. Interiors need: whitewashed and timber walls, plank and stone floors, hearths, beds, shelves, counters, barrels, sacks, hanging herbs, and mill machinery. Constraints and limits are in `design/region-meadowmere.md`.
